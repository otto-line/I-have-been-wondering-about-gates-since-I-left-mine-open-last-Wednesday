I have a story of a woman who carried a gate on top of her VW polo for 25 years. She has promised that she would do so until she found her home. At the age of 40 she found love, ten years later she put the gate to rest again a crumbled old bar, somewhere in the desert. Every gate is made of the same shapes. every gate is black with wrought iron. The shape is a heart. It reminds me of home. Every street i walk the gates follow me. I cannot seem to shake them.
I want the hearts to go away. the likeness of gates is the only way to forget them. Why are they all the same? A heart is a shallow sign of love.
100 year old gates are the same <3 as empty of text messages?
The same as general adorations of love for cities.
